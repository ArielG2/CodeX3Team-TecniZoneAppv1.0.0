# **`PB-01: Device Registration and Unique Identification`** 

- **`Type:`** `Functional Requirement` 

- **`Task:`** `Capture and store detailed device information (type, brand, model, serial number, and reported faults) in the database using a structured intake form.` 

- **`Project Application:`** `Creates the initial system record whenever a customer drops off a device at the shop. It assigns a unique identifier that prevents data duplication and serves as the baseline technical profile for the entire repair lifecycle.` 

# **`PB-02: Strategic Service Status Management`** 

- **`Type:`** `Functional Requirement` 

- **`Task:`** `Provide an interface featuring a static dropdown menu with predefined status options:` _`Under Diagnosis`_ `,` _`Under Repair`_ `, and` _`Listed`_ `.` 

- **`Project Application:`** `Enables technicians to manually update the active maintenance phase of each device. This standardizes the internal workflow and ensures the system reflects the true real-time progress of every job.` 

# **`PB-03: Delivery Planning and Commitment`** 

- **`Type:`** `Functional Requirement` 

- **`Task:`** `Integrate a visual calendar component into the registration form to select the estimated completion date for the service.` 

- **`Project Application:`** `Establishes clear turnaround timelines for technicians and provides customers with precise expectations regarding when their equipment will be ready, improving overall shop organization and customer satisfaction.`

# **`PB-05: Historical Tracking and Filtered Order Search`** 

- **`Type:`** `Functional Requirement` 

- **`Task:`** `Display a general list of registered jobs with filtering capabilities by customer name or device serial number.` 

- **`Project Application:`** `Allows administrative and technical staff to quickly locate past or active repair orders, eliminating the need to manually browse through disorganized spreadsheets or paper records.` 

# **`PB-06: Critical Inventory Management and Low-Stock Alerts`** 

- **`Type:`** `Functional Requirement` 

- **`Task:`** `Manually log the available quantity of spare parts and enforce a business logic rule that displays a warning text message when stock levels drop below 3 units.` 

- **`Project Application:`** `Prevents unexpected stockouts of essential components in the workshop, enabling timely restocking to avoid operational delays or paused repair jobs.` 

# **`PB-07: Cost Structuring and Automated Calculation`** 

- **`Type:`** `Functional Requirement` 

- **`Task:`** `Provide manual entry fields for labor costs and parts costs, automatically processing the calculation to display the total amount on screen.` 

- **`Project Application:`** `Eliminates human errors and inconsistencies associated with manual financial calculations, ensuring that the final charge shown to the customer is precise and transparent before closing the order.` 

# **`PB-08: Customer External Status Lookup Portal`** 

- **`Type:`** `Functional Requirement` 

- **`Task:`** `Design a simple search screen where customers can enter their order number to check the real-time status of their equipment.` 

- **`Project Application:`** `Offers real-time transparency to end users and significantly reduces the burden of in-person visits or phone inquiries regarding repair progress.` 

# **`PB-09: Monthly Management Consolidated Reporting`** 

- **`Type:`** `Functional Requirement` 

- **`Task:`** `Compile and export a plain text report or summary list detailing the total money collected and the total number of repairs completed within the month.` 

- **`Project Application:`** `Equips TECNIZONE's management with an analytical tool to evaluate business profitability, monitor monthly repair volume, and make data-driven operational decisions.`
