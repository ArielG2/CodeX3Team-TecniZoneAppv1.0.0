Currently, TECNIZONE manages its sales and technical service operations using static Excel spreadsheets and manually filled templates, an outdated methodology 
that negatively impacts the business's efficiency. This operational approach generates several critical deficiencies, including constant data duplication, 
manual calculation of costs, and disorganization in spare parts inventory, as stock levels are tracked without low-unit alerts. Furthermore, there is a lack 
of clear communication with customers regarding repair progress, as well as an inefficient registration of physical warranty checkmarks during reception. 
Finally, the dispersion of information across separate files prevents the simple consolidation of monthly repair counts and collected revenue, making the 
development of a centralized application essential to record device entries, track job statuses, manage basic spare parts alerts, and offer simple status 
lookups for customers.
